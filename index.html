Frequently Asked Questions

Is BoxaFlix completely free to download and use? +
Yes! BoxaFlix is 100% free on Google Play. You have full access to movie discovery, trailers, streaming guides, and personal watchlists with zero subscription fees.
Does BoxaFlix stream or host full movies? +
No. BoxaFlix is an official cinema discovery, trailer, and streaming search companion powered by The Movie Database (TMDb) API and YouTube. We direct you to verified legal streaming providers (Netflix, Disney+, Prime Video, Apple TV, etc.) where content is legitimately available.
Do I need to register or create an account? +
Never. BoxaFlix operates without accounts. Your bookmarks, watched lists, and ratings stay securely on your device, ensuring maximum privacy and instant speed.
Which Android devices are supported? +
BoxaFlix supports all modern Android devices running Android 8.0 (Oreo) and above, including smartphones, foldables, and Android tablets.

Privacy Policy & Terms

Official policy statement compliant with Google Play Developer Policy and GDPR regulations.

1. Information Collection & Zero-PII Policy

BoxaFlix does not require user registration. We do not collect or transmit personally identifiable information (PII) such as your name, physical address, email address, or billing data. All user-generated watchlists and diary notes are stored strictly locally on your Android device.

2. Third-Party APIs & Services

  • The Movie Database (TMDb) API: Used to display movie synopses, posters, and cast metadata. BoxaFlix uses the TMDb API but is not officially certified or endorsed by TMDb.
  • Google AdMob: We show ads to keep the application free. AdMob may process non-personal device identifiers (Android Advertising ID) and diagnostic metrics per Google's Privacy Policy.
  • YouTube Player: Used for official trailers. Interactions are subject to YouTube Terms of Service.

3. User Rights & Data Erasure

You can erase all locally stored watchlists and ratings at any time by clearing application data in Android Settings ➔ Apps ➔ BoxaFlix ➔ Clear Data.

4. Developer Contact
